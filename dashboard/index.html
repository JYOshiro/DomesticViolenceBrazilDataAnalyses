CAPSTONE DASHBOARD / BRAZIL

Recorded violence, alcohol indication, and reporting context.

This dashboard keeps health notifications, protection reports, alcohol survey indicators, beer-market signals, and policy markers separate. It is designed for descriptive interpretation, not causal claims.

PRIMARY QUESTION

What do Brazil’s health-notification and protection-reporting systems show about the recorded burden of violence, and where is alcohol documented as a risk indicator?

METHODOLOGY CHANGE

Vigitel continuity and comparability

  • 2022: no collection.The Ministry of Health reports that the contracted collection operation was interrupted; this dashboard displays a gap, not a zero.
  • 2024: changed collection context.The survey used a reduced, concentrated collection period, expanded mobile interviewing, and updated weighting based on the 2022 Census. Trend comparisons need caution.
  • 2024 current drinking: deliberately unavailable.The supplied data no longer populates the legacy 30-day field. It does contain past-12-month use and frequency fields, but they are not substituted because that would create a non-comparable estimate.
  • What remains comparable.The heavy episodic (abusive) consumption series remains displayed when the source response and survey weight are available.
