CAPSTONE INVESTIGATION / BRAZIL / 2012–2026

I started with alcohol. The data changed the question.

This investigation began by asking whether beer consumption and taxation could explain domestic-violence reports. Each test exposed a limitation, changed the next question, and ultimately shifted the goal from explaining why violence occurs to anticipating when recorded demand may be higher.

NEW QUESTION

Can calendar and recent reporting patterns predict higher notification volumes?

FINAL PIVOT

Prediction instead of causation.

Rather than claiming to know what causes domestic violence, two tree-based models test whether calendar context and earlier notification volumes can help services anticipate higher recorded demand.

IN SIMPLE TERMS

The models say that tomorrow’s notification volume usually resembles recent comparable days.

The strongest signal is the number recorded on the same weekday last week, followed by the same weekday four weeks earlier. Weekends, holidays, and other calendar information make smaller adjustments. The model predicts how many notifications may be recorded—it does not predict who will experience violence or explain why violence happens.
